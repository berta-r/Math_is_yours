Split a total into equal groups or find how many groups fit.

## Common difficulty
Mixing up the total, the size of a group and the number of groups.

## Hint strategy
Identify the total and what must be equal.

## Hints
- Identify the total and what must be equal.
- Draw equal groups and share the total between them.
- Multiply your proposed result by the divisor to check the total.
