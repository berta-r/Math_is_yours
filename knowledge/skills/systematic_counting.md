title: Systematic counting
prerequisites: ["arithmetic"]
---

List valid possibilities in an organised way without omissions or duplicates.

## Common difficulty
Counting the same case twice or overlooking a restricted case.

## Hint strategy
Choose one feature to keep fixed while you list cases.

## Hints
- Choose one feature to keep fixed while you list cases.
- Use a table or small tree to organise the possibilities.
- Check that every listed case satisfies the rules and appears only once.
