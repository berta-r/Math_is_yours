Combine quantities and find differences while keeping track of what each quantity represents.

## Common difficulty
Adding when an amount is taken away, or forgetting which total is being compared.

## Hint strategy
Name the starting quantity and describe what changes.

## Hints
- Name the starting quantity and describe what changes.
- Sketch the quantities before and after the change.
- Check whether your result should be larger or smaller than the starting amount.
