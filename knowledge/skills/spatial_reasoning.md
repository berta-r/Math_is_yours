Track shapes, orientations and parts under movement or a change of viewpoint.

## Common difficulty
Confusing reflection with rotation or losing track of hidden parts.

## Hint strategy
Choose a distinctive corner, edge or mark to follow.

## Hints
- Choose a distinctive corner, edge or mark to follow.
- Keep that feature fixed in your mind as you turn or combine the shapes.
- Compare several features, not just the overall outline.
