Describe a repeated unit or changing rule and extend it consistently.

## Common difficulty
Extending the last visible step without checking the whole pattern.

## Hint strategy
Describe what stays the same and what changes.

## Hints
- Describe what stays the same and what changes.
- Look for the shortest repeating block or a consistent difference.
- Test your proposed rule on more than one step.
