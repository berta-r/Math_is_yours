Distinguish boundary length from covered area and use appropriate units.

## Common difficulty
Using an area formula when a boundary length is requested.

## Hint strategy
Decide whether the question asks about the boundary or the inside.

## Hints
- Decide whether the question asks about the boundary or the inside.
- Label the known lengths and find one missing length at a time.
- Check your units: length uses units and area uses square units.
