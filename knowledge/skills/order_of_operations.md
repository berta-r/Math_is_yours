title: Order of operations
prerequisites: ["arithmetic","multiplication"]
---

Evaluate expressions using grouping and multiplication before addition.

## Common difficulty
Evaluating every operation from left to right regardless of priority.

## Hint strategy
Look for brackets and multiplication before calculating.

## Hints
- Look for brackets and multiplication before calculating.
- Evaluate one high-priority operation and rewrite the expression.
- Check that every operation was used exactly once.
