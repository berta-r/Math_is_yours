title: Proportional reasoning
prerequisites: ["multiplication","division"]
---

Preserve a multiplicative relationship when quantities scale.

## Common difficulty
Adding the same amount instead of applying the same scale factor.

## Hint strategy
Identify the two quantities that change together.

## Hints
- Identify the two quantities that change together.
- Compare a single group with the requested number of groups.
- Check that the same scale factor connects both quantities.
